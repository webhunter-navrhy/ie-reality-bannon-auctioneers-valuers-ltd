We strongly encourage you to visit no 1 The fairways, a beautifully appointed new home.In an exceptional development of only 9 luxury homes, with unrivalled amenities. An Attractive, A rated, newly built two-storey 4 bedrooms detached home with a contemporary finish, featuring a warm brick façade, rendered upper walls, dark tiled roof and modern windows.The Property includes a spacious paved driveway, landscaped frontage, private rear garden and paved patio area, offering excellent kerb appeal and low-maintenance outdoor space.

Ideally located on Drumree Road, the property is close to an excellent selection of primary and secondary schools, including Dunshaughlin Community College. Dunshaughlin offers a wide range of shops, cafes, healthcare and community services, together with parks, playgrounds and extensive sporting facilities, making it particularly attractive location for families.
